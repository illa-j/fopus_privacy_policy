Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Fopus mobile application (the “App”) for Android processes, why, with whom it is shared, how long it is kept, and how to delete it. It covers the App distributed through Google Play under the identifier com.fopus.aichatassistant.

In short
  • Fopus is a client for chatting with large language models. We have no server of our own: your requests go directly from your device to the Puter service, under your own Puter account.
  • Your chat history, attachments and the reports you submit are stored only on your device.
  • Data reaches only the third parties needed to run the service: Puter (models and files), Google AdMob (ads for users without a subscription), RevenueCat and Google Play (subscriptions).
  • We use no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. We do not sell personal data.

1. Who we are

App: Fopus (com.fopus.aichatassistant)
Developer: Fopus

The controller of the data described here is the developer of the App — Fopus (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question, including access and deletion requests, contact xepodev@gmail.com. This page is hosted on fopusprivacypolicy.vercel.app.

Legal bases (for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland): performance of our contract with you — providing the App’s functionality; your consent — personalised advertising; legitimate interest — security and abuse prevention.

2. Data we process

2.1. Signing in to Puter (third party)

Chatting requires signing in to your own Puter account. The sign-in happens on Puter’s own page inside an embedded browser window in the App. We never see or store your password, email address or name — the App receives only a “signed in” signal. Session files for that sign-in live in the storage of that window on your device. If you sign in through a third-party provider (for example Google), that happens on Puter’s side and is governed by that provider’s policy. You can sign out at any time from the App’s Settings.

2.2. Messages and attachments

The text you type and the files you attach (photos or documents) are sent to the model through your own Puter account. We run no server that receives, stores or processes your conversations. Attachments are uploaded into your Puter file system; a photo can be taken with the camera or chosen from the gallery — the camera permission is requested only when you tap “Take a photo”, and oversized images are resized on the device before sending. On the device, the conversation (with its attached images) is kept in the App’s private storage so you can reopen it.

2.3. Advertising ID and ads (Google AdMob)

For users without a subscription, the App shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner above the composer, an interstitial (no more than once every 10 minutes when a new chat is started) and an optional rewarded video that temporarily unlocks a flagship model. Before initialising the ads SDK we ask for your consent via Google UMP; if consent is not given, no ad request is made. Subscribers see no ads at all, and no ad is shown on the very first launch or before you have sent a message.

As our ad provider, AdMob may receive your device’s advertising ID, general device information and your IP address in order to serve and measure ads. You can turn off personalised advertising in your Android settings (Google → Ads → delete or reset your advertising ID), and you can withdraw consent by writing to xepodev@gmail.com. Starting a subscription removes advertising entirely.

2.4. Subscriptions and payments (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions are purchased through Google Play Billing, and subscription state is processed by RevenueCat. We never see or store your card details or any other payment credential — Google processes the payment. RevenueCat receives an app user identifier and your subscription state (start, renewal, cancellation, refund) so that a Premium entitlement can be verified. On the device we keep only the result — subscribed or not — so that Premium is honoured offline. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play.

2.5. Data that stays on the device

Chat history and attachments, response reports (reason, optional note, the response text and a timestamp) and your settings (language, theme, selected model, temporary unlock state) are kept in the App’s private storage on your device. We have nowhere to send them: there is no developer server, and reports are never transmitted.

2.6. Public model catalogue

The App may fetch a small public JSON file listing the available models. That request contains no user data.

3. What we do not collect

The App does not collect or request: precise or approximate location, microphone input (the microphone permission is explicitly disabled), contacts, calendar, health or fitness data, financial information (card numbers), browsing history or the list of installed apps. There is no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the App. The only permission the App requests at runtime is camera access, and only when you tap “Take a photo”.

We may also disclose data where required by law or by a valid request from a competent authority. We do not sell personal data and do not share it for purposes other than those described above.

5. How long data is kept

  • Chat history, attachments, reports and settings — on your device until you delete them (see section 6) or uninstall the App. The App keeps no other copy, and we hold none at all.
  • Data in your Puter account, including uploaded files and the account identity itself — per Puter’s policy, for as long as your Puter account exists.
  • Purchase and subscription records — kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as their policies and tax or accounting rules require, and then removed. Deleting your account does not erase them, which is why they are listed again in section 6.
  • Advertising data (advertising ID, ad requests) — kept under Google’s policy; you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings at any time.

6. How to delete your data and your account

6.1. Delete your data, keeping your account

  1. In the App: Settings → Clear chat history. This deletes every stored conversation, the images attached to them and the reports saved from them; it takes a second tap to confirm.
  2. Files you sent to the models: attachments were uploaded to your own Puter file system. Delete them from your Puter drive at puter.com/dashboard.
  3. Everything else the App stored — settings, your language and model choice, the cached model list — is removed when you uninstall the App: it all lives in the App’s private storage on your device.

6.2. Delete your account

  1. Sign in at puter.com/dashboard with the same account you use in the App.
  2. Open your account settings there and delete the account, using Puter’s own deletion flow.
  3. That ends your access to the App (it will ask you to sign in again), removes the files stored in that Puter account, and removes the identity Puter holds for it.

We cannot delete a Puter account for you: it is your account with Puter, not ours, and we never receive your email address, username or password. If you have lost access to it, write to xepodev@gmail.com and we will point you to Puter’s own recovery and deletion process.

A subscription is not cancelled by deleting the account. Cancel it in Google Play, under your subscriptions — Google Play, not the account, is what bills it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the data we process, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent (for example for personalised advertising) and to receive your data in a portable format. To exercise any of these, email xepodev@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children below the minimum age in your country (generally 13, and 16 in the EEA). The App’s ad request configuration likewise does not declare a child-directed audience. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

All transmission to external services (Puter, AdMob, RevenueCat, Google Play) uses HTTPS/TLS. Your password never reaches the App. Note that the App’s local data (chat history and reports) is not additionally encrypted on the device; it lives in the App’s private storage, protected by Android.

10. International transfers

The providers listed in section 4 may process data outside your country, including in the United States, on the basis of their own contractual safeguards.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the App’s features or legal requirements change. The date of the latest revision always appears at the top of this page. Material changes will be announced in the App or in the release notes on Google Play.
